Why this matters

反馈压力 usually reflects the controller-visible signal, often after filtering or scaling.

原始压力 usually exposes more tooth ripple and sensor noise, which is better for calibrating pulsation and measurement noise.

A

Fit feedback pressure first

Use 反馈压力 as the primary target. Best when you care about matching the controller-facing signal.

B

Fit raw pressure first

Use 原始压力 as the primary target. Best when you care about tooth ripple, quantization, and sensor-side noise.

C

Fit both as a two-layer model

Treat 原始压力 as plant-plus-sensor output, then derive 反馈压力 as a filtered/scaled published signal.
